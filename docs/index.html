Can Google Calendar subscribe with one click?

Google Calendar does not offer a universally reliable, official one-click flow for arbitrary public ICS subscriptions. Our button opens its add-by-URL screen; paste the link shown above to finish.

Will changes appear immediately?

Not necessarily. The source calendar is regenerated periodically, but Apple and Google independently decide when to refresh a subscribed feed. Do not rely on this service for last-minute changes.
