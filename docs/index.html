Getting started

Make room for
what matters.

01
Choose your calendar

Use Apple Calendar to open a subscription directly, or open Google Calendar's “From URL” settings.

02
Add the subscription URL

For Google Calendar, paste the link shown here and select “Add calendar”. On a Mac, choose iCloud as the subscription location to share it with your Apple devices.

03
Stay in the loop

Calendar clients periodically check for updates. For important classes and exams, always verify official announcements.

One link. Every class.

Use this public URL in any calendar app that supports subscribing to an iCalendar (ICS) feed. Subscribing keeps the calendar connected; downloading and importing the file only creates a static copy.

https://d2hp-calendar.yuhaohe.com/calendar.ics
Google Calendar: Settings → Add calendar → From URL → paste this link. Google may take hours to reflect feed updates.
Does Google Calendar subscribe in one click?

Google Calendar does not provide a reliably supported, universal one-click confirmation link for arbitrary ICS subscriptions. The Google button opens its add-by-URL page; paste the URL above to complete the subscription.

Why can't I edit a class or set individual alerts?

Subscribed calendars are generally read-only. This feed intentionally includes no fixed reminders. For custom notifications, use your calendar's available settings or copy a class to a personal calendar.

Is this an official university timetable?

No. This is an independent student-developed utility. Always confirm changes, locations and examinations through official Université Paris-Saclay communications.
